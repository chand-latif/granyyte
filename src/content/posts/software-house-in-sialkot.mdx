## What to ask any software company in Sialkot before you hire

There are a lot of software companies in Sialkot now, and the good ones won't mind these questions. Ask whoever you're talking to, us included, before you pay anything:

1. **Can I see work that's live right now?** Real App Store links or a working website. Screenshots in a PDF don't count.
2. **Who will actually write the code?** Meet them. If the person selling isn't the person building, find out who is.
3. **Do I own the source code when it's done?** You should get the full code, the database and the accounts. A vague answer here is a reason to walk away.
4. **Is the price fixed against a written scope?** Open-ended hourly billing on a loose brief is how small projects quietly turn into expensive ones.

We'll answer all four in the first meeting, without you having to push.
